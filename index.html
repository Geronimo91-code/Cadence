Cadence

A training week that is built for you and rebuilt every Sunday.

Tell Cadence how you train, what you're aiming for, and when. It plans your week, you log what you did, and it adjusts.

Weekly plan built around your club practices and your deadline
Log sets, loads and how it felt, straight from the session
Every Monday: feedback and a new week, from what you actually did
Daily calorie and protein targets that follow your training days

Cadence uses AI models (Google Gemini or OpenRouter) to write your plans, reviews and meal estimates, and stores your profile, plans and logs in a Firebase database. If you would rather not have your training data processed by AI or stored online, please don't use Cadence.
