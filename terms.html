What Cadence is

A training planner that uses AI models to write weekly plans, reviews and nutrition estimates from what you tell it and what you log. It is built and run by an individual, offered free and as is.

Not medical or professional advice. Cadence is not a doctor, physiotherapist, dietitian or qualified coach, and the plans it produces are generated by a machine. Training carries risk of injury. Check with a qualified professional before starting, especially if you have an injury, a health condition, are pregnant, or are returning from a long break. Stop if something hurts. You train at your own risk, and you are responsible for your own decisions.

Your account

  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Keep your sign-in safe.
  • You must be 16 or older.
  • Don't misuse the service: no attempts to break it, no scraping other people's data, no abusive content in names, notes or club feeds.

Clubs

You can belong to up to five clubs. Coaches of any club you join can see your plans, logged sessions and reviews, and can add team sessions to your week. They cannot see your nutrition or weight. Anyone with the invite code can join a club, so treat the code as semi-public and remove members you don't recognise. Leave a club at any time from the Club tab.

AI output

Plans, reviews, coaching cues and calorie estimates are generated automatically and will sometimes be wrong, generic or badly calibrated for you. Meal estimates in particular are rough guesses from a description or a photo. Use judgement, adjust anything that doesn't fit, and don't treat the numbers as measurements.
