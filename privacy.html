Privacy

Last updated 12 September 2026

Cadence is a training planner run by an individual, not a company. This page explains what it stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. Plain language, no legal padding.

What is stored

DataWhy
Email address and sign-in identityTo let you back into your account (handled by Google Firebase Authentication)
Profile: name, age, sex, height, weight, sports, level, goals, available days, equipment, injuries or limits, deadline, languageTo build training plans that fit you
Plans, logged sessions, weekly reviewsThe core of the app
Weight history, and, if you enable nutrition, daily targets and the meals you logNutrition targets and progress charts
Push subscription and time zone, if you turn reminders onTo send the daily reminder
Club membership and role, if you join a clubCoach access and the club feed

Where it goes

  • Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) stores your account and data.
  • Vercel hosts the app and runs its server functions.
  • AI model providers — Google Gemini, and OpenRouter as a fallback — receive the parts of your profile and logs needed to write a plan, a weekly review or a meal estimate. Photos you send for meal estimation are processed by the same providers and are not stored by Cadence.

Cadence does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not share anything with anyone else.

Who can see your data inside the app

  • You — everything.
  • Coaches of any club you join — your plans, logged sessions and weekly reviews, so they can coach you. If you belong to several clubs, every one of their coaches sees the same training picture, including sessions that belong to your other clubs. Never your nutrition, your meals or your weight.
  • Other members of your club — only the sessions you finish, and only while you keep yourself visible in the club feed and leaderboard. You can hide yourself at any time in the Club tab.

Leaving a club ends coach access immediately.

Your choices

  • Export — Profile → Export my data gives you everything as a JSON file.
  • Delete — Profile → Delete my account erases your profile, plans, logs, meals, weights and club membership, and your sign-in account. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Nutrition is optional; turn it off in Edit setup and nothing about food is stored.
  • Reminders can be switched off, which deletes the push subscription.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct, restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. The export and delete buttons cover access and erasure directly; for anything else, get in touch.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. There is no automatic expiry, because training history is more useful the longer it runs.

Children

Cadence is not intended for anyone under 16. If a minor plays in your club, they should use it through a parent or coach account.

Security

Traffic is encrypted. Database rules restrict every document to its owner, with the narrow coach exception above. No system is perfect; please don't store anything here you would be upset to lose or reveal.

Contact

Questions, corrections or deletion requests: use the contact address shown in the app or in the repository this is built from.
